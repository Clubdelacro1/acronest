FAQ

Good
to know

Do I need previous experience?

No. Acronest welcomes every level, from people taking their first steps to advanced practitioners. Tell us about your practice when you register and we will plan the schedule around it.

Can I come without a partner?

Of course — many people come on their own. If you come with a partner, check the Promo Duo prices.

What is included in the ticket?

All activities, accommodation, daily meals, snacks and drinks (coffee, tea). Meals: Wednesday dinner; Thursday to Saturday breakfast, lunch and dinner; Sunday breakfast and lunch.

How do I pay?

A €100 deposit by bank transfer secures your place. The remaining balance can be paid in cash on the first day of the event or in 3 installments.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable, but they are transferable to someone else until the day before the event (updated to the current price).

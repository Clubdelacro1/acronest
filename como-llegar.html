How to
get there

The Cortijo is high up in the Alpujarra mountains, near a small village called Cástaras. The town with the best bus connections is Órgiva. Here is a summary of the easiest ways to arrive.

Cortijo Casería del MercadoCortijo Casería del Mercado S/N, 18438 Las Alpujarras, Granada, España
Open in Google Maps ↗

Check-in: Wednesday 7 July from 4 pm · Check-out: Sunday 11 July at 5 pm. Plan to arrive early: the last stretch is a mountain road.

🚗By car

Search for “Cortijo Casería del Mercado. Finca ecológica con alojamiento rural” in Google Maps. Parking is on the left at the first entrance when you arrive at the Cortijo.

  • From Granadaapprox. 1 h 30 min
  • From Málagaapprox. 2 h

Coming by car and have free seats? Tell us and we'll connect you with people who need a ride.

🚌By bus

The bus stop with the best connections to the Cortijo is Órgiva. Torvizcón and Trevélez are closer but have fewer buses. All these routes are run by Alsa: we recommend buying your ticket in advance.

  • Granada → Órgivaapprox. 1 h 30 min · several buses a day
  • Granada → Torvizcónfewer departures
  • Málaga → Granada → Órgivaapprox. 2 h + 1 h 30 min
  • Málaga → Motril → Órgivafew departures

From Órgiva or Torvizcón to the Cortijo: taxi (approx. 30–40 min) or ask us and we'll help you find a ride.

If you can't get to Málaga bus station before 3 pm, your best option is the direct bus from Málaga airport to Granada. There are many more departures from Málaga to Granada.

✈️By plane

The closest airport is Granada (GRX), about 1 h 30 min away. Málaga (AGP) is at a similar distance but has fewer connections to Órgiva. Try to book a flight that lands early.

  • Granada airport → Granada bus stationairport bus · 20–45 min · leaves about 30 min after each flight lands
  • Málaga airport → Málaga bus stationbus every 20–30 min · approx. 20 min

🚕Taxi or local driver

Ask us and we'll help you book a local driver — cheaper than a regular taxi, and a great option if you share it with other participants. From Málaga, Granada or Órgiva.

🚐Shuttle

We will only organise a shuttle if at least 15 people need it and can't use other transport. You can ask for it until 30 June. The price depends on how many people join.

Write to us in advance and we'll help you with anything you need to get here.
